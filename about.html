🪿 About AppleGeese 🍎

AppleGeese is a collection of goose-themed browser games and projects, mostly brought to life by AI. Pick a game and start playing — no install required.
  • Apple Goose Farm — manage your orchard and geese
  • Goose Game — grow the longest neck and beat your high score
  • Idle Goose Game — click, gather apples, and prestige your way to goose glory
  • Orchard Warfare — command your flock in a goose-themed real-time strategy game
  • Honk Defense — place goose towers and defend the orchard nest from fox waves
This website was created with the use of AI.
